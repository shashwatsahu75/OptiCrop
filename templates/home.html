🌱 The N-P-K Equilibrium
  • Nitrogen (N): Leaf growth & vegetative green biomass.
  • Phosphorus (P): Root elongation, tillering, and flowering.
  • Potassium (K): Cell turgor, pest defense, and drought resilience.
🧪 Why Soil pH Dictates Yield

Even with abundant fertilizer, extreme soil pH locks nutrients into insoluble compounds. Most cultivars achieve peak nutrient uptake between 6.0 and 7.5.
